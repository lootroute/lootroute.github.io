GTA 6 Guide: Money, Fastest Cars & Hidden Tricks

5 free tips below. The full guide adds early money by chapter, 10 fastest cars with locations, robbery prep and police escapes.

5 free tips

1Your wallet caps at $20,000

Once you carry $20,000 you can't pick up more, and you lose carried cash when you die. Bank it at an ATM after every big job.

2Focus Mode shows loot

Press both sticks together (L3 + R3 / LS + RS). Enemies glow red, usable items yellow and cash green. It also picks up nearby voices that lead to random encounters.

3Dark blue buildings can be entered

On the map, dark blue buildings have a way in (door, vent or window). Light blue ones are closed. Rotate the map to spot entrances.

4Your car door is a weapon

Press Square / X while driving to swing the door into bikers chasing you. On a motorbike the same button kicks.

5Save before every mission

There is no mission replay, so save first if you want a second try.
