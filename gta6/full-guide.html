GTA 6 Full Guide

Thanks for unlocking. Bookmark this page – we add new tips every week.

1. Early money, chapter by chapter

Chapter 1 (Jason)

  • Check the mailbox at Jason's house. Story pay lands there – small amounts from Donnie and Brian after the first missions.
  • Search the side rooms in the hidden lab during "Lab Fees". The shelves can take you from almost nothing to around $600.
  • Grab Donnie's stash bag in "Don Gone": open the trunk of his car and take the bag ($1,200). You also keep his revolver.
  • Finish "Buck Me" for a $100 welcome bonus – it also unlocks ATM deposits.
  • Spend before Chapter 1 ends. Weapons carry over to Chapter 2, so turn spare cash into gear.

Chapter 2 (Lucia)

  • Robbery spree: waffle house, liquor stores, thrift store and convenience stores in one afternoon can push you past $2,000. Lose your wanted stars between hits.
  • Read the store's security level on the map before you rob it. Low-security stores are fine solo; medium and high are easier with both characters.
  • Sell clean cars to Moses (Watkins Auto Parts). Older, plain cars without a tracker and in good condition sell best. Only the first registration is free.
  • Optional "Coffee Break" jobs: the bowling alley safe and stash are worth about $2,500 in total; sell the stash bag to a fence.
  • Collectible cards can be sold at pawn shops.

Chapter 3 and later

  • Banks become worth it once you get the dye-pack counter in Chapter 3. Before that, a single dye pack can ruin the take.
  • Bank job routine: roomy getaway car, sticky explosives, quiet entry through a back door or garage, blow the safe, leave before police arrive. Don't rush the cash drawers at reception – that triggers the alarm right away. A good score is around $15,000–$20,000.
  • Gang hideouts restock. Clear them for drugs, marked bills and rare cars, sell to fences, and come back later.

2. Mistakes that cost money

  • Dying with cash or loot. You lose carried cash and both stash bags (and a bag left in a car that blows up or gets impounded).
  • Crashing a lot. Repairs can cost up to about $2,000 each.
  • Hitting banks too early (dye packs before Chapter 3).
  • Ignoring hourglass missions. Missions marked with an hourglass disappear after a while.

How to keep a car

  • Scan it first: hold Up on the D-pad, open the Waink app and point it at the car. You'll see its security, resale value and registration cost.
  • Avoid advanced trackers early – they alert police if you drive too far. They can be dealt with from Chapter 3.
  • Register the car (Pay 'n' Spray, or Moses from Chapter 2) and call it later with the Scooter Bros app.
  • Some garages need the Key Cloner, which unlocks later in the story.

4. Hidden tricks the game never explains

  • Shove vehicles: walk into a car or boat and keep walking, then hold Square to push. Flips a car back over or pushes a beached boat into the water – no tow needed.
  • Vehicle menu: hold Left on the D-pad, then push the left stick up for hazards, high beams, windows and convertible roofs.
  • Any explosion opens a safe – grenades and propane tanks work, not just sticky bombs.
  • Swim faster: click the left stick on the surface (twice for max). When stamina runs out, dive – underwater uses lung capacity instead.
  • Auto-drive: hold Down on the D-pad for Cinematic Mode and follow the prompts. Your character drives to the waypoint obeying traffic – great for staying low-key with heat.
  • Secret radio station: at The Vault nightclub in Vice City, scan the pink gas-mask poster with your phone camera to unlock The Vault Radio.
  • Hidden difficulty options: Settings > Gameplay has Accessible Combat and Reduced Vehicle Damage at the bottom. They don't affect trophies.
  • Fast travel workaround: book a RydeMe car and pay $100 extra to skip the ride. Switching characters is free.
  • Buffs: eating, sleeping and showering give short boosts to health, stamina or focus.

5. Robbery prep & losing the police

  • Case the place: walk in as a customer, lock onto each guard and press Right on the D-pad to mark them.
  • Dress for the job: hat and bandana for the crime, then take them off. Keep a spare outfit in the trunk.
  • Police track your face, outfit, visible gun, car, CCTV and partner. Holster weapons, switch cars and destroy CCTV recordings.
  • Ammu-Nation early buys: suppressor (police respond later) and red-dot sight (click the right stick to aim in first person).
  • Watch your criminal profile: limb shots wound instead of kill. The worst level, "Irredeemable", can't be undone.

6. Train your stats

  • Health: weightlifting (press both triggers fast, release slowly, finish the set before stamina runs out).
  • Stamina: running, cycling, treadmill.
  • Focus: fishing or pool – and Focus gains don't drop if you stop training.
